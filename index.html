Field guide

Help & controls

Movement & combat

WASD / Arrow keys move · Mouse aim · Click / Space shoot · Shift dash · E shield · 1–4 switch unlocked guns.

Survive

Watch your health and lives. A shield protects you briefly after respawning. If eliminated, spectate living players; use +/- to zoom.

Pickups & scoring

Collect tokens for points, a heart for healing, a pink heart for an extra life, a cartoon gun to upgrade, or a blue invincibility star for brief protection. The giant golden star is worth 50 points and drops when its carrier is eliminated.

Arenas & teams

The host chooses team count, bot skill, match length and lives. The arena is randomly selected from the map carousel when the match starts.

Lobby & rooms

Create a Firebase room, share its five-character code, and wait for friends to join. Only the host can start or pause the match; the host's game state is synced to every player.

Account & progress

Sign in with Google to save your profile and view the shared XP leaderboard. Cloud accounts require Firebase Authentication and a configured Realtime Database.

About Chaos Arena

Made for mayhem

Chaos Arena is a browser-based top-down arena shooter built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Canvas API. Its themed maps, bots, pickups, and local map workshop are designed for quick, chaotic matches.

Google sign-in, cloud XP, and friend lobbies use Firebase. The room host runs the match simulation and shares its state through Realtime Database.
